Release discipline

Nothing graduates on marketing momentum.

  • Stable means proven: deterministic tests, holdouts, rollback, source identity, and lane-boundary checks must pass.
  • Preview means preview: incomplete privacy, utility, or production evidence stays visibly labeled and operationally blocked.
  • Public remains useful: core local continuity and retrieval do not become bait for a paid control surface.
  • CLI remains primary: dashboard, HTTP, and MCP extend the product; they do not replace the shortest reliable agent path.
