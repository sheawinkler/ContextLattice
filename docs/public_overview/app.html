Domain strategy

Public routing (high-level)

  • contextlattice.io -> public site and documentation.
  • app.contextlattice.io -> premium app, auth, and billing experience.
  • Internal routing, callback handlers, and service lanes are private operator config.
Launch posture

Near-term vs later

  • Now: app domain handles auth, billing, entitlement state, and paid console.
  • Later: optionally serve the lightweight app/lite runtime surface from the same domain.
  • Always: keep premium operations on app-domain infrastructure only.
App domain split reduces ops confusion during paid launch. Use premium launch gates before enabling production entitlements.
