# Refund Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-18

## Request window

Refund requests are reviewed case by case. Submit a request within 14 days of
purchase with the transaction reference, purchase date, plan, workspace
identifier, and reason for the request.

## Payment rails

- Approved card and PayPal refunds return through the original payment provider
  and remain subject to its settlement timing.
- Cryptocurrency payments require manual transaction verification and may take
  longer because the original network settlement cannot be reversed.
- Fraudulent, abusive, or policy-violating usage is not eligible for refund.

## Contact

Send requests to `hello@contextlattice.io` with the subject
`ContextLattice refund request`.

Signed order forms or negotiated commercial agreements control when their
refund terms differ from this policy.
